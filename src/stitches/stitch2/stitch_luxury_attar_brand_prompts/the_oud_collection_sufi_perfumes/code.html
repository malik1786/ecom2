The Alchemy of Oud

Extracted from the heartwood of the Aquilaria tree only after it has been touched by a specific mold, Oud is nature’s ultimate act of transformation. Our atelier sources only the most ancient, resin-dense heartwood from the dense forests of Southeast Asia.

Each composition in this collection is a meditation on darkness and depth, designed to wear close to the skin and unfold over hours.
